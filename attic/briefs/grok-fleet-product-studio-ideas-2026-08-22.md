# Grok Research Brief: One Materially Better Idea for Claude Fleet Product Studios

You are advising on **Claude Fleet**, a local control plane for autonomous coding-agent work in
isolated git worktrees and tmux sessions. This is not a request for a generic multi-agent framework
or a list of popular plugins. Look for one or a few mechanisms that would materially improve real
product outcomes.

## What Fleet is

Fleet coordinates Programs, Project-MAIN sessions, bounded worker tasks, model/harness routing,
isolated lanes, verification, landing, deployment, audits, evidence, and owner attention.

- The **owner** confirms product direction, taste, scope growth, promotion, external effects/cost,
  land, deploy, and release.
- A **Project MAIN** owns one confirmed Program end to end. Inside that scope it autonomously
  decomposes work, chooses workers/models, creates capped pending rows inside its occupant-bound own
  Program and repository, releases them as a separate act, reviews evidence, retries or repairs
  ordinary failures, and continues until a named owner boundary.
- A **worker** owns one bounded act and exclusive write-set, proves it, commits it, and reports.
- A **Supervisor** observes health and stalls but is not a second command hierarchy.
- The **dispatcher is deliberately mechanical**: it starts only tasks that Project MAIN released.
  It does not interpret product intent or become another AI control plane.

Current harnesses include Claude/Fable/Opus, Codex/Sol, and Pi with GLM-5.3 through a bounded
adapter. Anonymous free OpenCode Ox Alpha has also completed a native OpenCode review plus Pi
canaries covering anonymous transport, real repository read tools, zero-cost usage records, and
exact session continuation through Pi's existing `opencode` provider. The remaining gap is a
process-isolated Fleet Pi profile and its lifecycle telemetry. Model names are routing hypotheses,
not permanent roles.

## Product Studio workflow being calibrated

The first clean calibration is a standalone ambitious 3D tower-defense survival shooter. It is not
a continuation of prior games. The Studio currently uses:

1. a shared project contract and bounded context packs;
2. parallel independent systems-loop and visual/technical-art workers;
3. targeted repair when a precommitted falsifier fires;
4. integration by Studio-MAIN;
5. a fresh cross-family critic;
6. explicit owner gates for architecture/slice, visual anchors, playable tasting, weapon roster,
   and measured VFX budget;
7. deterministic scenarios, structured state, logs, screenshots, motion captures, and telemetry as
   recoverable evidence. A green build is never visual or gameplay proof.

The intended reusable organization is a small **Product Studio core** plus thin domain/platform
overlays such as GameDev, web/Three.js, iOS, Roblox, or Steam. It must not become a universal
harness, permanent agent army, generic context registry, or second task system.

## Observed problems and boundaries

- Long sessions are dominated by model-visible tool-result input tokens.
- TUI prompt delivery and readiness have caused real failures; structured engine/RPC modes may be
  better than terminal inference.
- Context packs are valuable for cross-game reuse only when facts, hypotheses, proposals, unknowns,
  triggers, freshness, proof, and transfer predicates remain explicit.
- Excess process can delay the first playable; insufficient criticism produces polished but hollow
  demos.
- Creative and visual work needs resident iteration sometimes, while blind critics must remain
  fresh.
- Game feel and visual identity remain owner taste; credentials, paid assets, signing, submission,
  deployment, and other external effects remain explicit boundaries.
- We want autonomous studios, not autonomous product promotion.

Prompt-development lessons already measured through a lightweight **Sharpen** approach:

- mine session/project context before adding instructions;
- compile for the actual destination: lean for the current session, self-contained for a fresh
  executor;
- dose discipline by the expected failure mode and executor strength;
- keep context and constraints, strip generic ceremony;
- give structured work an output shape and each compound slice its own done evidence;
- require a final argument against the recommendation;
- prefer requirement alignment over feature volume.

## Question

What is the smallest materially better concept, mechanism, or workflow change you would introduce
to Claude Fleet for autonomous **GameDev or iOS Product Studios**? You may also reject the premise
that a new mechanism is needed.

Do not optimize for novelty, model count, plugin count, or theoretical autonomy. Optimize for:

- faster arrival at an honest playable/usable increment;
- better gameplay feel, visual/UX quality, and causal product coherence;
- fewer unnecessary owner interruptions without weakening owner authority;
- exact recoverable evidence;
- model/harness interchangeability;
- reuse of earned knowledge across products without importing stale product decisions;
- lower total tokens, cost, rework, and operational failure.
