# Cross-studio delta ledger (kit v2) — size-capped, human-promoted

One entry per transferred lesson: (studio, failure mode observed, mitigation promoted,
applicability predicate, trail citation). A lesson transfers ONLY if its failure mode exists
under the permanent constraints AND the mitigation is a mechanical check — otherwise it stays in
the studio's own decision record. Entries are folded into the kit templates or retired (with a
"retired because…" note) by owner promotion; the ledger must stay readable in one sitting — it is
a delta list, never a rulebook. A new MAIN ingests the kit + this ledger, nothing older.
